Privacy Policy for Cats Radar

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Cats Radar respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Cats Radar mobile application accesses, stores, collects, and processes, and how that information is used.

1. Information stored locally on your device

Cats Radar allows you to record encounters with cats and keep a personal history of those encounters.

Depending on how you use the app, locally stored information may include:

  • the location of a cat encounter;
  • photos that you choose to attach;
  • cat characteristics, such as coat information;
  • encounter details;
  • locally generated statistics and history.

This information is stored locally on your device.

Cats Radar does not operate a developer-managed server for storing your cat encounter history.

Your encounter photos and the precise coordinates associated with individual encounters are not intentionally uploaded to Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Firebase Remote Config.

2. Location information

Cats Radar may request access to your device's location when you choose to record or edit the location of a cat encounter.

Precise location may be used to:

  • save where a cat encounter happened;
  • display encounters on a map;
  • group encounters by geographic area;
  • generate location-based statistics and history.

The precise coordinates associated with an encounter are stored locally on your device.

Cats Radar does not intentionally send the precise coordinates of your cat encounters to Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Remote Config.

Cats Radar does not use your precise encounter location for advertising.

Cats Radar does not access your precise location in the background unless a future version of the application explicitly introduces such functionality and this Privacy Policy is updated accordingly.

Google Analytics for Firebase may independently derive approximate geographic information, such as country, region, or city, from network information such as a masked IP address. This approximate information is separate from the precise location stored for an individual cat encounter.

3. Photos

Cats Radar allows you to optionally attach photos to cat encounters.

The app accesses only photos that you choose to add or create through functionality provided by the application.

Photos attached to encounters are stored locally on your device.

Cats Radar does not intentionally send those photos to Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, or the developer's servers.

The app may record an analytics event indicating that a photo was attached and the general method used to attach it. This event does not contain the photo itself.

4. Analytics

Cats Radar uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the application is used and to improve its functionality and usability.

Cats Radar may send analytics events describing general application actions, such as:

  • viewing a particular application screen;
  • recording a cat encounter;
  • setting or clearing a cat coat category;
  • attaching a photo;
  • manually setting an encounter location;
  • importing photos;
  • deleting or restoring records;
  • exporting or importing a backup;
  • starting or ending a walk;
  • aggregate counts related to certain actions.

Analytics events created by Cats Radar contain predefined categories, boolean values, counts, durations, and application actions.

They do not intentionally contain:

  • precise encounter coordinates;
  • photos;
  • encounter identifiers;
  • user-entered place names;
  • encounter timestamps;
  • names;
  • email addresses;
  • phone numbers;
  • message contents.

Google Analytics for Firebase may also automatically collect certain technical and usage information required to provide analytics services. This may include:

  • app interactions;
  • session information;
  • app instance identifiers;
  • device or other identifiers;
  • application version;
  • device and operating system information;
  • approximate geographic information derived from network information;
  • application lifecycle events.

This information is used to understand application usage, identify usage patterns, and improve Cats Radar.

5. Crash reporting and diagnostics

Cats Radar uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes, errors, and technical problems.

When the application crashes or encounters certain technical failures, Crashlytics may automatically receive diagnostic information such as:

  • crash stack traces;
  • application state at the time of the crash;
  • device model;
  • operating system version;
  • application version;
  • installation identifiers;
  • technical diagnostic information related to the crash.

This information helps identify and fix application stability problems.

Cats Radar does not intentionally include your encounter photos or precise encounter coordinates in Crashlytics reports.

6. Firebase Remote Config

Cats Radar uses Firebase Remote Config to configure certain application features and behavior without requiring a new version of the application to be installed.

Firebase Remote Config may process technical information necessary to provide this functionality, including information such as:

  • application identifier;
  • application version;
  • operating system and platform version;
  • language;
  • time zone;
  • country code;
  • Firebase installation identifier;
  • other technical application and device information required by Firebase.

Remote Config is not used by Cats Radar to upload your cat encounter history, encounter photos, or precise encounter coordinates.

7. Firebase and Google services

Cats Radar uses services provided by Google LLC, including:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase;
  • Firebase Crashlytics;
  • Firebase Remote Config;
  • supporting Firebase infrastructure required by these services.

Technical and analytics information may therefore be transmitted to and processed by Google as necessary to provide these services.

Google's handling of information is governed by its applicable privacy and data-processing terms.

8. How information is used

Information collected through Firebase services is used to:

  • understand how Cats Radar is used;
  • improve application functionality and usability;
  • identify crashes and technical problems;
  • improve application stability and performance;
  • measure the use of application features;
  • configure or gradually introduce application functionality;
  • diagnose problems affecting users.

The developer does not sell personal information collected through Cats Radar.

Cats Radar does not use your cat encounter photos or precise encounter locations to create advertising profiles.

9. Data sharing

Cats Radar does not sell your personal information.

Technical, diagnostic, and analytics information may be transmitted to Google as necessary for Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, and related Firebase infrastructure to operate.

The developer does not intentionally share your locally stored:

  • cat encounter photos;
  • precise encounter coordinates;
  • cat encounter history.

10. Accounts and personal information

Cats Radar does not require you to create an account.

You are not required to provide information such as:

  • your name;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • password;
  • postal address.

Cats Radar does not currently provide a user account system.

11. Backups and data import/export

Cats Radar may allow you to export or import application backups.

Backup files are created or selected as a result of an action initiated by you.

The contents of backup files may include information stored in Cats Radar.

Cats Radar does not intentionally upload the contents of those backup files to Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Remote Config.

If you choose to copy, upload, share, or store a backup using another application or cloud storage provider, the handling of that backup is governed by the privacy practices of the service you choose.

Analytics may record that a backup was exported, imported, or rejected, together with general result categories or counts. The backup contents themselves are not included in those analytics events.

12. Data security

Information transmitted between Cats Radar and Firebase services is protected using encrypted network connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

Information stored locally by Cats Radar is subject to the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable measures are used to limit unnecessary collection and transmission of user information.

13. Data retention and deletion

Cat encounter information stored locally on your device remains there until you:

  • delete individual records through Cats Radar;
  • clear the application's data through Android settings; or
  • uninstall the application and its locally stored data is removed by Android.

Information previously transmitted to Firebase services may remain for a period of time according to the retention settings and policies applicable to those Firebase and Google services.

Uninstalling Cats Radar therefore does not necessarily immediately delete analytics or diagnostic information that was previously transmitted to Firebase.

14. Application permissions

Cats Radar may request Android permissions necessary to provide application functionality.

Depending on the features you use, these may include permissions related to:

  • device location;
  • photos, media, or image selection;
  • camera access, if you choose to take a photo through the app.

Permissions are requested only when relevant to the corresponding functionality.

You can deny or revoke permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

Some Cats Radar features may not work if the required permission is not granted.

15. Children's privacy

Cats Radar is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly request children to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other account information.

If the application's intended audience or data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Cats Radar, Firebase services, applicable requirements, or the way information is processed.

When material changes are made, the updated version will be published at the privacy policy URL associated with Cats Radar.

The date at the top of this document indicates when the Privacy Policy was last updated.
